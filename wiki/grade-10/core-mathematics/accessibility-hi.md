# Accessibility guidance for the HI mathematics corpus

The supplied document is titled `Core Mathematics Grade 10 - HI 2025.pdf`. `HI` is retained
as source metadata; its exact institutional meaning and the preferred Kenyan Sign Language
(KSL) terminology must be confirmed by an educator familiar with the source edition. The
rules below are ElimuTayari implementation guidance, not an official KICD accessibility
standard.

## Use this profile when generating a teaching pack

- Put every instruction in writing and pair words with the mathematical symbol, diagram, or
  coordinate rule. Do not make an audio cue the only way to receive information.
- Use captions or a written transcript for video demonstrations. If an interpreter is present,
  leave a clear line of sight and do not place essential notation behind the interpreter.
- Introduce one new term at a time in a visible glossary. Preserve the English term, the
  symbol/formula, and the teacher-approved KSL sign or explanation; never invent a KSL sign.
- Make diagrams high contrast, uncluttered, and labelled in the same order as the written
  explanation. For transformations, show before/after figures and the invariant property.
- Keep units, direction, and operation names visible throughout a worked example. Use colour
  only as a supplement; pair it with labels or line styles for learners who cannot rely on
  colour differences.
- Replace listening-only activities with a visible board, card sort, signed/written peer
  exchange, or hands-on model. Confirm understanding through a written or signed response.
- Ask the teacher to check pace, interpreter access, seating, lighting, and learner preference
  before using the pack. Accessibility is individual and should not be inferred from the label.

## LLM response rules

When `accessibility_profile=HI` is requested:

1. Give the written explanation first, then the diagram/notation and worked steps.
2. Offer captions/transcript and a visual alternative for any video or spoken activity.
3. Avoid claims about KSL vocabulary unless a teacher supplies the approved term.
4. Keep examples short, numbered, and easy to point at during signed instruction.
5. Ask whether the learner wants English-only, English plus KSL glossary, larger print, or a
   more visual step-by-step format.

## Review checklist

- [ ] A mathematics educator and HI-accessibility specialist reviewed the terminology.
- [ ] Every activity has a written/visual path and does not depend on hearing.
- [ ] KSL signs are teacher-supplied or linked to an approved resource.
- [ ] Diagrams, captions, contrast, and notation were checked with actual learners.
