# Grade 10 Core Mathematics

This is the ElimuTayari LLM wiki for the Grade 10 Core Mathematics learning area in the
Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) pathway.

## What this learning area is for

Core Mathematics builds numerical, algebraic, spatial, graphical, statistical, and
problem-solving fluency. It connects classroom mathematics to decisions learners meet in
Kenyan homes, schools, roads, farms, businesses, and future technical careers.

## Curriculum shape

The design has three strands and fourteen sub-strands:

| Strand | Sub-strands | IDs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numbers and Algebra | Real Numbers; Indices and Logarithms; Quadratic Expressions and Equations | `M-ALG-01` to `M-ALG-03` |
| Measurements and Geometry | Similarity and Enlargement; Reflection and Congruence; Rotation; Trigonometry 1; Area of Polygons; Area of a Part of a Circle; Surface Area and Volume of Solids; Vectors I; Linear Motion | `M-GEO-01` to `M-GEO-09` |
| Statistics and Probability | Statistics I; Probability 1 | `M-STA-01` to `M-STA-02` |

## Suggested learning arc

1. Establish number sense and algebraic representation.
2. Move from similarity and transformations into trigonometry and measurement.
3. Apply the geometry toolkit to vectors and motion.
4. Finish with data, variation, probability, and evidence-based decisions.

This arc is a pedagogical suggestion, not a claim that KICD mandates one term-by-term
sequence. The graph labels such links as `pedagogical_prerequisite`.

## General outcomes carried through every page

Learners should increasingly be able to compute accurately, represent ideas in multiple
ways, reason and explain, use digital tools responsibly, solve real problems, collaborate,
and make decisions that respect people and the environment. Each sub-strand page names the
most relevant CBC competencies, values, and pertinent and contemporary issues (PCIs).
