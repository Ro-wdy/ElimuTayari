# Strand 1.0: Numbers and Algebra

**Teacher lookup:** Grade 10 → Core Mathematics → **Strand 1.0 Numbers and Algebra**.

## Strand focus

This strand develops number classification and operations, index and logarithmic notation,
and algebraic expressions and equations. It supports accurate calculation, symbolic
reasoning, and modelling of familiar quantities.
